Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Currency Converter app (package com.hayhak.currencyconverter) handles information. The developer is Hayhak.

1. Overview

The App converts currencies and shows live rates, history charts, statistics, and optional rate alerts. No account is required. There are no ads and no advertising tracking.

2. Data the App processes

  • Exchange rates: Live rates from ExchangeRate-API. Historical charts from Frankfurter (ECB data). Only currency codes and date ranges are sent.
  • Local storage: Rates, favorites, alarms, language, theme, and last-used pairs stay on your device.
  • Amounts you enter stay on the device.
  • Notifications: A local system notification may appear for rate alerts.
  • App updates: Google Play In-App Updates when installed from the Play Store.
  • Analytics: Google Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage events (screen views, language). No amounts, names, or advertising IDs are sent. Advertising ID collection is disabled.

3. Third parties

  • ExchangeRate-API: live rates (base currency code only).
  • Frankfurter: historical rates (currency codes and date ranges).
  • Google Play: update checks when installed from the Play Store.
  • Firebase Analytics: anonymous usage events to improve the App (no ads, no Advertising ID).

We do not run our own servers for your App data. We do not sell your data.

4. Retention

Local data remains until you change it or uninstall the App.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.
