Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Currency Converter app (package com.hayhak.currencyConverter) handles information. The developer is Hayhak.

1. Overview

The App converts currencies and shows live rates, precious metals, history charts, statistics, a home-screen widget, and optional rate alerts. No account is required. There are no ads and no advertising tracking. Advertising ID is not collected. The App does not use location, contacts, microphone, or camera.

2. Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: to download rates and metal prices.
  • Notifications (optional): for rate alerts. Android 13+ asks for permission.
  • External storage (Android 9 and below only): if you export history as CSV.

3. Data the App processes

  • Exchange rates: Live rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). Historical charts from Frankfurter (ECB data). Only currency codes and date ranges are sent.
  • Precious metals: Gold, silver, platinum, palladium from Gold-API, with jsDelivr / Fawaz currency-api as fallback. Sent: metal symbol or a generic USD table.
  • Local storage: Rates, favorites, alarms, language, theme, and widget settings stay on your device.
  • Amounts you enter stay on the device and are not sent to the APIs.
  • Notifications: A local system notification may appear for rate alerts.
  • Widget: Shows rates stored on the device; does not send user data to a separate server.
  • Analytics: Google Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage events (screen views, language, currency codes). The SDK may collect technical data (device, Android version, app-instance ID, approximate country). No amounts, names, or Advertising IDs are sent.
  • App updates: Google Play In-App Updates when installed from the Play Store.
  • Feedback (optional): An email draft may include version and device info; it reaches us only if you send it.
  • Share and CSV: User-initiated; not uploaded to Hayhak servers.
  • Device backup: If Android backup is on, App data may be backed up to your Google account. Hayhak cannot access it.

Network requests use HTTPS.

4. Third parties

  • ExchangeRate-API: live rates (base currency code only).
  • Frankfurter: historical rates (currency codes and date ranges).
  • Gold-API: metal prices.
  • jsDelivr / Fawaz currency-api: fallback metal prices.
  • Google Play: update checks when installed from the Play Store.
  • Firebase Analytics: anonymous usage events to improve the App (no ads, no Advertising ID).

We do not run our own servers for your App data. We do not sell your data. Rate and analytics requests may go to the providers’ servers (including outside the EEA).

5. Retention and deletion

There is no account. Local data remains until you change it or uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes App data from the device. For Firebase records, email thunderbascraft@gmail.com.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.
