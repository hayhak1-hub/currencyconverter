Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android app Currency Converter (package com.hayhak.currencyconverter, “the App”) handles information.

Developer / data controller: Hayhak
Contact: thunderbascraft@gmail.com

1. Overview

The App converts currencies and shows live rates, precious metals, history charts, statistics, a home-screen widget, and optional rate alerts.

  • No account is required. There is no sign-in or user profile.
  • There are no ads and no advertising tracking.
  • Advertising ID is not collected (disabled in the App).
  • The App does not use location, contacts, microphone, camera, or a list of other apps.

2. Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: to download rates and metal prices.
  • Notifications (optional): for rate alerts. On Android 13+ the system asks for permission; you can refuse. Without it, alerts cannot show notifications.
  • External storage (Android 9 and below only): if you export history as CSV. Android 10+ uses MediaStore; this permission is not requested.

3. Data the App processes

  • Exchange rates: Live rates come from the free ExchangeRate-API open endpoint (open.er-api.com). Historical charts use the Frankfurter API (ECB data, frankfurter.app / api.frankfurter.dev). Only currency codes (and date ranges for history) are sent — not your name, email, or an identifier we assign to you.
  • Precious metals: Gold, silver, platinum, and palladium prices come from Gold-API (api.gold-api.com), with jsDelivr Fawaz currency-api as fallback. Sent: a metal symbol (e.g. XAU) or a generic USD table request — not personal information.
  • Local storage: Rates, favorites, alarms, language, theme, widget settings, and last-used pairs are stored with Room and DataStore on your device only.
  • Amounts you enter stay on the device and are not sent to us or to the rate APIs.
  • Notifications: If you set a rate alert and grant permission, the App may show a local system notification. No cloud account is used for this.
  • Widget: The home-screen widget shows rates stored on the device; it does not send user data to a separate server.
  • Analytics: Google Firebase Analytics collects usage events to improve the App. Events we log: screen name, language tag, converted currency codes (not amounts). The SDK may also collect technical data: device model, Android version, app version, approximate country, and an app-instance ID. Advertising ID is not collected. Details: Firebase privacy.
  • App updates: If installed from Google Play, the App may check for updates via the Play In-App Updates API.
  • Feedback (optional): If you open email from Settings, the draft may include app version, manufacturer, model, and Android version. That text reaches us only if you send it.
  • Share and CSV: Sharing a conversion or exporting history as CSV is initiated by you and is not uploaded to Hayhak servers.
  • Device backup: If Android backup is on, App data (favorites, alerts, settings) may be backed up to your Google account. Hayhak cannot access that backup.

Network requests use HTTPS.

4. Third parties

  • ExchangeRate-API (exchangerate-api.com): live rates.
  • Frankfurter (frankfurter.app): historical rates.
  • Gold-API (gold-api.com): metal prices.
  • jsDelivr / Fawaz currency-api: fallback metal prices.
  • Google Play: distribution, update checks, and Play services. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): anonymous usage events. Firebase privacy.

We do not run our own backend servers for your App data. Rate and analytics requests may go to the providers’ servers outside the EEA (including the United States).

5. Sharing and sale

We do not sell your data. Favorites, alerts, and amounts are not uploaded to Hayhak servers. The parties above only receive the technical requests needed for rates, metal prices, updates, or analytics.

6. Retention and deletion

There is no account, so account deletion does not apply. Local data remains until you change it in the App or uninstall. Uninstalling removes App data from the device.

Firebase analytics records follow Google’s retention periods. For a deletion or information request, email thunderbascraft@gmail.com.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR or KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to processing. Because we do not keep user accounts or server-side profiles, deletion is typically done by uninstalling the App.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s behaviour changes. The current text is on this page; the date is shown above.
