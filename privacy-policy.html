Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android app Currency Converter (package com.hayhak.currencyconverter, “the App”) handles information. The developer is Hayhak.

1. Overview

The App converts currencies and shows live rates, history charts, statistics, and optional rate alerts. No account is required. There are no ads and no advertising tracking.

2. Data the App processes

  • Exchange rates: Live rates come from the free ExchangeRate-API open endpoint. Historical charts use the Frankfurter API (ECB data). Only currency codes (and date ranges for history) are sent — not your name, email, or an identifier we assign to you.
  • Local storage: Rates, favorites, alarms, language, theme, and last-used currency pairs are stored with Room and DataStore on your device only.
  • Amounts you enter stay on the device and are not sent to us.
  • Notifications: If you set a rate alert and grant permission, the App may show a local system notification. No cloud account is used for this.
  • App updates: If installed from Google Play, the App may check for updates via the Play In-App Updates API.

3. Third parties

  • ExchangeRate-API (exchangerate-api.com): live rates.
  • Frankfurter (frankfurter.app): historical rates.
  • Google Play: distribution, update checks, and Play services when installed from the Play Store.
  • Firebase Analytics: anonymous usage events (screen views, language). No ads and no Advertising ID collection.

We do not run our own backend servers for your App data.

4. Sharing and sale

We do not sell your data. Favorites, alerts, and amounts are not uploaded to Hayhak servers. Third parties above only receive the technical requests needed for rates or updates.

5. Retention

Local data remains until you change it in the App or uninstall. Uninstalling removes App data from the device.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.
