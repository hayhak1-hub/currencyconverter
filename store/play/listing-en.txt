## Full description (max 4000)
Convert currencies instantly with live exchange rates, beautiful charts, and tools for everyday use and travel.

FEATURES
• Converter — enter an amount, pick currencies, swap with one tap
• Live rates — dashboard for your base currency with favorites
• Statistics — daily change and comparison with yesterday
• History — any currency pair from 1 day to 5 years, export CSV
• Rate alerts — get notified when your target rate is reached
• Precious metals — gold, silver, platinum, palladium
• Home-screen widget — live rates at a glance
• 20 languages — including English, German, and Turkish
• Dark mode and Material You dynamic colors

DATA SOURCES
Live rates from ExchangeRate-API (free tier). Historical charts from Frankfurter (ECB data). Rates are cached on your device for offline viewing of recent data.

PRIVACY
No account required. No ads. See the in-app privacy policy for details.

Made by Hayhak.
